DevRadar

One scan of your whole developer program

Developers now meet your product through agents, search and other people's code as often as through your own site. DevRadar reads your docs the way an agent, a developer and a competitor would, and puts everything it finds in one report.

Free. No account. About 90 seconds. Search powered by Bright Data.

What the scan checks

Everything in one report

Every scan covers all of this. There is nothing to pick and nothing to install.

Agent readiness

Whether AI agents can use your docs: robots.txt rules for GPTBot, ClaudeBot and other AI crawlers, what Chrome, ClaudeBot, GPTBot and curl each get back, llms.txt, OpenAPI specs, an advertised MCP server, and whether a missing page returns a real 404.

Docs and sample code

Your docs the way a new developer meets them: whether they load over valid TLS, a quickstart, code samples, working search, how fresh the pages are, and broken links.

Competitors

The companies in your category, found from your own site and search, with their docs run through the same checks as yours.

Developer personas

Who your developers are likely to be: roles, stacks and what each of them needs from your docs.

Targeting and messaging

Where to reach those developers and what to say to them, drawn from the personas and your category.

Events and speakers

Upcoming conferences, meetups and hackathons that match your platform, from public calendars, and who is speaking about your space.

Communities

Your official community channels, and the meetups and user groups around your category.

Buzz on X

What developers are saying about you and your competitors on X, compared side by side.

Integrations and adoption

Where you show up in the places agents and developers install from: MCP registries, Claude connectors, IDE marketplaces and agent frameworks, plus adoption on GitHub.
