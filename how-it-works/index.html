How it works

Save the company once. Run the work from there.

DevRadar is a home for DevRel apps. Sign in, pin a company, and each app starts from the same profile.

1. Sign in

GitHub, Google, or a magic link to your work email. No password. We never post on your behalf.

2. Scan your company

Paste a landing page. DevRadar reads the public site, respects robots.txt, and finds docs, API reference, GitHub and community links.

3. Confirm the profile

Fix anything we got wrong, then save. Saving a company does not claim ownership. Anyone can track any public company.

4. Open an app

Event Scanner and Deep Scan run today. The other apps are on the way. Join a waitlist and we will email you when one opens.
