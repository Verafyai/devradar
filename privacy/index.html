Privacy

The scan needs no account. It stores the URL you scanned and what it found on public pages, so the report can be read again from its link.

Scans identify themselves as DevRadar, respect robots.txt, and stay within a request budget for each domain. We do not collect private contact details of developers.

If you give an email to unlock a full report, we store that email with the report.

Reports stay unlisted. We do not publish who failed a scan.
