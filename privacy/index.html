Privacy

DevRadar stores the account you sign in with (email, name, and the avatar a provider sends) and the company profiles you save. A company profile is built from public pages. Saving one does not claim that you own that company.

Company scans identify themselves as DevRadar, respect robots.txt, and stay within a page cap. We do not collect private contact details of developers. People-related apps, when they ship, use public professional profiles only.

Waitlist emails are stored so we can tell you when an app opens. Magic links expire after 30 minutes. Session cookies are httpOnly.

Reports from Deep Scan stay unlisted. We do not publish who failed a scan.
