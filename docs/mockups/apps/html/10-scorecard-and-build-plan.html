15 day build-out

One app at a time, in order of value and dependency. Every day runs the same loop.
D1FoundationsCompany profile, app shell, run engine with step status, credits, eval harness, golden set of 10 companies
D2Deep ScanReport redesign, lenses, top 5 fixes, share and rescan diff
D3Event ScannerSources, CFP deadlines, fit score with reasons
D4Event ScannerPlan, budget, exports (.ics, CSV, Notion), weekly watch
D5Docs & CodeCrawler, link check, accuracy vs OpenAPI, agent readiness
D6Docs & CodeSandbox sample runs, agent quickstart run, Open PR
D7CompetitionGrid from Deep Scan + Docs runs, battle cards, change alerts
D8Social TrendsTopic clustering, deltas, share of voice, angles, Monday email
D9Dev ProfilerSignal collection, persona clustering, quotes, messaging matrix
D10InfluencersRelevant reach, ranking, profile drawer, outreach drafts
D11Idea GeneratorIdeas from all apps, outlines, starter repos, calendar
D12LooperGitHub app, release webhooks, drift detection
D13LooperFix PRs with sample runs, weekly report, guardrails
D14SEO + copyProgrammatic pages, free tools, schema, copy pass on all 9
D15Re-score + launchFull eval, fix regressions, move apps to LIVE that pass the gate

The daily loop

1
Build
Ship the day's scope behind a flag
2
Eval
Run against the 10 golden companies
3
Score
9 categories, auto + judged
4
Fix
Top 3 failures, same day
5
Check
Screens at 375, 768, 1440
6
Log
Score history, LOG entry

Gates

✓LIVE needs a score of 75+, Output trust 7+, no hard fails
✓Every claim in a result links to its evidence
✓Every run shows real steps; no silent hangs
✓No result over its promised time at p90
✓Nothing posts, emails or opens a PR without a click
